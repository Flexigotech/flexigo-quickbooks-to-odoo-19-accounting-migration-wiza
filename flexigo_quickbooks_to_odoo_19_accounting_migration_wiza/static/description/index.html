Before you buy: this app uses a paid external service

New connections with QuickBooks Online work through FlexigoTech Cloud, an external service of FlexigoTech (Flexibles y Accesorios Gobe, S.L., Spain) that runs on our servers, outside your Odoo. Your Intuit app's client secret is kept encrypted on our servers, and each request your Odoo makes to QuickBooks Online goes through FlexigoTech Cloud, which sends it on with a QuickBooks access token that it gets and renews with your Intuit app, and returns the answer. FlexigoTech Cloud also watches the sync and emails you if something fails.

You need your own QuickBooks Online account, under the terms of QuickBooks Online. The subscription pays for our service (the connection, the monitoring and support), not for access to QuickBooks Online.

For Odoo 20.0 · QuickBooks Online

Move your QuickBooks Online books into Odoo 20, reconciled

Extract your QuickBooks Online data, load the master data, post one opening entry from your Trial Balance with every open invoice and bill, bring in the stock at the cut-off date, and compare Odoo with QuickBooks account by account before you go live.

For finance teams and Odoo partners who switch a company from QuickBooks Online to Odoo 20 at a cut-off date. It moves balances and open items, not the history of every transaction.

1. Extract

Reads your QuickBooks Online company page by page, through FlexigoTech Cloud, into a staging area in Odoo (requires FlexigoTech Cloud subscription).

2. Load

Turns the staging into partners, products, accounts, payment terms, analytic accounts, one opening entry and the opening stock. Runs inside your Odoo.

3. Reconcile

Compares Odoo with QuickBooks account by account, open item by open item and item by item. A difference of more than 0.01 blocks Reconciled.

What you need

  • A QuickBooks Online company. From QuickBooks Desktop, only its lists are read, from an IIF export.
  • Your own Intuit app, created in your Intuit Developer account: its client ID and secret are what connects (see the Intuit terms tab).
  • The FlexigoTech Migration Base module, installed with this one, and FlexigoTech Cloud, the free base module of the subscription.
  • For the extraction, the FlexigoTech Cloud trial or subscription (Settings → FlexigoTech Cloud).

What runs where

FlexigoTech Cloud keeps the client secret of your Intuit app and the QuickBooks tokens encrypted, exchanges the authorization code, renews the access and passes each call of a new connection on to QuickBooks Online, keeping it only for the time needed to deliver it.

Your Odoo keeps the staging, the mappings and everything loaded: the load, the opening entry, the stock and the reconciliation run in Odoo and do not need the subscription. What is already extracted or loaded stays in Odoo.

Extraction from QuickBooks Online (requires FlexigoTech Cloud subscription)

Extract on a migration run reads, 1,000 records a page: accounts, tax codes, terms, classes, departments, customers, vendors, items, open invoices, open bills, every credit memo and vendor credit, and the Trial Balance to the day of the extraction, with the Inventory Valuation Summary at the same date. Everything goes into the migration staging of the project.

If FlexigoTech Cloud stops answering or the service ends, the run pauses where it was and goes on by itself when it can. The staging is never deleted.

Master data load

Load on a migration run creates the records in the company of the project, archived when they are inactive in QuickBooks. Loading again updates the same records and never creates again what you deleted.

  • Accounts are matched to your chart by the mapping set of the project, else by the account number; a new account is created only when the mapping gives its code.
  • Customers and vendors become partners; inventory, non-inventory and service items become products, with their income and expense accounts once those are loaded.
  • Terms with due days become payment terms, with their early payment discount.
  • Classes and departments become analytic accounts in the plan of the dimension mapping.
  • A tax code is never created as a tax. Date-driven terms and unmapped accounts, classes and departments are listed in the run instead of being guessed.

One opening entry from the Trial Balance

The Trial Balance becomes one posted opening entry at its end date, in the first general journal of the company: one line per account with its balance, and on the receivable and payable accounts one line per open invoice or bill, with its customer or vendor, its number, due date and open balance, in its own currency when it has another. You match those lines with the payments in Odoo.

No invoice or bill is created, so no tax or income is counted twice. A Trial Balance that does not balance creates nothing, and loading again duplicates nothing.

Credit memos and vendor credits

Unapplied credit memos and vendor credits are open items of the opening entry too, with the opposite sign, their customer or vendor, and their own currency at their exchange rate, so the receivables and payables match QuickBooks. The fully applied ones give no line.

Stock at the cut-off date

The Inventory Valuation Summary gives the quantity and the asset value of each inventory item. They go into the main warehouse of the company, on the products the items became, at the QuickBooks value. They come from a location of their own, "QuickBooks opening stock", without a valuation account, so no stock valuation entry is made: the inventory account keeps the balance of the opening entry.

An item that is not loaded as a storable product, or is below zero, is listed and no stock is loaded. Loading again moves nothing twice.

Reconciliation, account by account

After the load, Odoo's balance at the Trial Balance date is compared with QuickBooks' for every account, each receivable and payable account with its open items, and the stock item by item. The differences go to the reconciliation of the project, and a difference of more than 0.01 blocks Reconciled.

Connections and access

Each QuickBooks connection and its staging stay in their company. Only members of the Migration Manager group manage connections. A copy of the database (neutralized) forgets the QuickBooks tokens, and uninstalling the module deletes the Intuit apps kept in FlexigoTech Cloud. Connections made with a version before 1.1.0 keep working as bought, directly from Odoo (Connect to QuickBooks and Test Connection); to extract, you create a new connection, which goes through FlexigoTech Cloud.

The Intuit app is yours

  • You create the app in your own Intuit Developer account and accept the Intuit Developer Terms of Service as its owner. FlexigoTech Cloud calls QuickBooks Online with that app, for you.
  • Intuit shows the production keys of an app only after it approves the App Assessment Questionnaire, which asks, among other things, for the addresses of your license agreement and privacy policy, your domain and the regions where the app is hosted.
  • You do not need to publish the app on Intuit's app store: a private app works with its production keys.
  • Intuit's terms make the app owner responsible for the service providers that act for them, and do not allow the data to be used for general archiving or back-up. FlexigoTech Cloud passes the calls on and keeps them only for the time needed to deliver them.

Intuit's limits and fees

  • Where Intuit's App Partner Program applies (today the US, Canada except Quebec, the UK and Australia), every app starts on its free Builder tier: 500,000 read calls a month for the workspace where the app lives, counted again on the 1st of each month. Above that, Intuit blocks the read calls until the next month, unless you move to one of its paid tiers. Calls to the sandbox and to the OAuth endpoints are not counted.
  • Intuit allows 500 requests a minute and 10 a second per company, and 1,000 records per query; the extraction reads 1,000 records a page.
  • Intuit may change its fees at any time. What Intuit charges is between you and Intuit: the FlexigoTech Cloud subscription does not include it.

How it is tested

We have not had access to an Intuit sandbox yet. The extraction, the load, the opening entry, the stock and the reconciliation are tested against a simulator that follows Intuit's official documentation and its official SDK. The names of the columns of the Inventory Valuation Summary (Qty and Asset Value) are not yet checked against a QuickBooks Online company. The test against a real QuickBooks Online company will be done when the sandbox is available.

Which QuickBooks does it read?

QuickBooks Online, through Intuit's API: everything on this page. From QuickBooks Desktop, the IIF export of its lists (File → Utilities → Export → Lists to IIF Files) uploaded on a File Upload connection: Test Connection says what it holds, and Extract and Load bring its accounts, classes, customers, vendors and items like those of QuickBooks Online. The opening entry, the open items and the stock come only from QuickBooks Online.

What needs the subscription, and what does not?

The extraction, from QuickBooks Online or a Desktop export, and connecting a new connection, need the FlexigoTech Cloud trial or subscription. The load, the opening entry, the stock, the reconciliation and everything already extracted or loaded work in Odoo without it.

An account, class or payment term is listed as unmapped. What do I do?

For an account, add an Account Type value to the mapping set of the project: as source, the Id, number or name of the QuickBooks account; as target, the Odoo account, or the code of a new account, which the load then creates. Without a mapping, the account is matched by its number. Classes and departments go in the dimension mapping of the project: an analytic account, the plan to create it in, or Do Not Migrate. Date-driven payment terms are not created: set up that payment term in Odoo yourself. Then run Load again: it updates what is there and duplicates nothing.

Does it import my old invoices and transactions?

No. It brings the balances at the cut-off date and the invoices, bills and credits that are still open, as lines of one opening entry. No invoice or bill is created, so no tax or income is counted twice. The history stays in QuickBooks.

What happens if the subscription ends in the middle of an extraction?

The run pauses where it was and nothing is deleted, in the staging or in Odoo. It goes on by itself when the service is back.

I bought an earlier version. What changes?

Your connections keep working as you bought them: Connect to QuickBooks and Test Connection from Odoo, with their tokens encrypted in Odoo. The extraction is new in 1.1.0 and goes through FlexigoTech Cloud, so it needs a new connection. To update, put the FlexigoTech Cloud module in the same addons path, restart and update this module (Apps → Upgrade); restarting without updating does not load it.

Does it work with several companies?

Yes. Each migration project loads into its own company, and each QuickBooks connection and its staging stay in their company.

Has it been tested with a real QuickBooks Online company?

Not yet: it is tested against a simulator that follows Intuit's official documentation and SDK (see the Intuit terms tab).

  • Intuit: your QuickBooks Online company, your Intuit Developer account, the App Assessment Questionnaire and Intuit's fees.
  • The migration project itself (the mappings, the cut-off date, checking the reconciliation) is done by you or your Odoo partner.

If a company uses the hosted whistleblowing channel of our flexigo_whistleblowing module, the person who reports writes on a public page of FlexigoTech Cloud, and we process what they send on behalf of that company, which is the controller and informs the person who reports (Annex IV of the data processing agreement). Each report, attachment and message is sealed on arrival with the company's key, so that we cannot read it, and it is deleted when the company's Odoo collects it. For each report, its dates, a fingerprint of the follow-up code and the messages sealed for the person who reported remain until 183 days after its arrival, or until it is collected if that takes longer. We do not store the IP address of the person who reports, neither in the application nor in the logs of that page. If the company leaves the service, the page accepts reports for 90 days more, what is pending can be collected for another 90 days, and then it is deleted.

In the Klaviyo connector, contacts, orders and events go directly from the customer's Odoo to Klaviyo and do not pass through our servers. There is one exception, so that the customer keeps meeting its legal duties: while the sync is paused because the database has no service, the unsubscribes and the erasures (Article 17 GDPR) made in its Odoo reach Klaviyo through FlexigoTech Cloud. The email address, phone number or Klaviyo profile id of that person then passes through our servers only in transit, during that request, without being stored or logged. We process it on behalf of the customer, as a processor.

The authorisation that connects our connector to the seller's Amazon account goes through our Odoo instance on Odoo.sh.

For data our connector obtains from the Amazon Selling Partner API, we also commit to:

  • using it only to manage and fulfil the seller's orders;
  • deleting buyers' personal data no later than 30 days after delivery, except the copy on a posted invoice, which the law requires us to keep;
  • encrypting it in transit and at rest and limiting access to those who need it;
  • reporting any security incident affecting Amazon data to Amazon within 24 hours of detecting it, and to the seller within the same period, so that, as controller, it can notify its supervisory authority where applicable (article 33 GDPR);
  • not sharing it with unauthorised third parties.
